App Title
LocalHelper — Final Refined Product Definition
One-Line Description
LocalHelper is a trusted hyperlocal platform that connects nearby people for short-duration physical help tasks, enabling users to quickly find reliable assistance while allowing helpers to earn flexible income on their own schedule.

Core Product Vision
LocalHelper is not a freelancing platform, delivery app, or home-service marketplace.
It is a:
“trusted nearby human assistance network.”
The platform is designed for situations where:
someone needs quick real-world help, 
does not want to depend on friends, 
and wants a fast, low-friction, trustworthy interaction. 
At the same time, it creates flexible earning opportunities for nearby people who are free for short durations.

Primary Problem Being Solved
User Problems
People frequently face small but urgent tasks where:
they need an extra person temporarily, 
nearby help is unavailable, 
asking friends feels uncomfortable, 
existing platforms are too expensive or too slow, 
local labor is unstructured and unreliable. 
Examples:
carrying luggage, 
shifting hostel items, 
pickup/drop assistance, 
grocery help, 
queue standing, 
temporary moving support. 

Helper Problems
Many students and local workers:
want flexible side income, 
do not want fixed part-time jobs, 
have free time between activities, 
want quick earning opportunities nearby. 
Current options:
require long commitments, 
fixed schedules, 
employer dependency, 
or social exposure. 
LocalHelper provides:
independent earning, 
flexible availability, 
local work, 
short-duration tasks.

User Roles
1. Task Seeker
A person who needs short-term nearby help.
2. Helper
A nearby person available to complete tasks for payment.
A user can switch between both roles anytime.

Core User Flow
Task Seeker Flow
Step 1 — Open App
User selects:
“I Need Help” 
Step 2 — Create Task
Required fields:
task title 
description 
task photo (optional but encouraged) 
estimated duration 
offered payment 
urgency level 
approximate area/location 
Step 3 — Nearby Helpers Notified
Available helpers nearby receive alerts.
Step 4 — Helper Accepts
User views:
helper profile, 
trust metrics, 
completion history. 
Step 5 — Mutual Confirmation
Both sides agree.
Exact contact/location unlocks only after acceptance.
Step 6 — Task Completion
Payment handled externally initially:
UPI 
cash 
Step 7 — Completion Feedback
Both sides rate interaction quality and confirm completion.

Helper Flow
Step 1 — Activate Availability
Helper toggles:
“Available Now”
Optional availability duration:
30 mins 
1 hour 
2 hours 

Step 2 — View Nearby Tasks
Helper sees:
task summary 
payment amount 
duration 
area 
seeker trust profile 

Step 3 — Accept Task
After acceptance:
contact unlocks, 
navigation assistance available. 

Step 4 — Complete Task
Helper receives payment directly.

Step 5 — Reputation Updated
Completion impacts:
completion rate, 
reliability metrics, 
trust indicators.
